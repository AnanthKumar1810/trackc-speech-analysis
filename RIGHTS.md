The machine-readable source register contains the exact original audio hashes, download URLs and source dates. These notices support redistribution for the educational dataset purpose; they are not a blanket assurance about every jurisdiction or unrelated commercial/promotional use. NASA's guidelines specifically distinguish publicity/privacy rights for identifiable people and prohibit implied endorsement. Do not use the package to suggest endorsement by any speaker, government agency, or archive.

All flawed recordings are alterations made by this dataset project. Credit the original speaker and archive for source audio and the dataset project for edits. Do not label them as authentic mistakes made by the speaker. No logos, identity cloning models, or voice synthesis are included.
